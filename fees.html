Important Notes — Bus Transport Fees AY 2025–26

Registration
  • You will need to fill in the bus registration form and get confirmation from the RBT office.
  • If the student did not use the bus service in AY 2025–26, a registration fee of AED 50 will be applicable.
Fee Structure & Installments
  • Transport fee (FS.2 to Year 13) is calculated for ten months in an academic year, payable in 3 term-wise installments.
  • 1st Installment — on or before 30th August 2026
  • 2nd Installment — on or before 1st January 2027
  • 3rd Installment — on or before 1st April 2027
  • There will be no refund for the unused portion of the term.
Routes & Availability
  • Buses operate on specific routes and may cover only some portions of the listed areas.
  • You are advised to confirm availability of the transport service with the Regal Bus Transport department before admission.
Payment Information
  • Accepted methods: Cash, Current-dated Cheque (payable to Regal Bus Transport LLC), Skiply Mobile App.
