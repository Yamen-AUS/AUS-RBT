About RBT

Dubai's Dedicated School Transport Partner

Regal Bus Transport (RBT) was established on 1st April 2009 with a singular purpose: to provide safe, reliable, and comfortable transport for school-going children in Dubai.

RBT has grown to operate a fleet of 81 RTA-certified buses (77 active), staffed by professional drivers and well-trained attendants. Our team excels in safety, routing, first aid, student management and public relations.

Our Mission

Safe, reliable, affordable transport for school children — fully RTA-compliant.

Our Vision

Dubai's most trusted independent school transport provider, setting the benchmark for safety.
